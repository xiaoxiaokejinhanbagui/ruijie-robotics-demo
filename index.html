ABOUT

Research Focus

I am currently studying at Nanyang Technological University, with a focus on reinforcement-learning-based whole-body robot control.

My current work explores symmetry-aware neural policies based on graph neural networks (GNNs), together with upper/lower-body decoupling for more fine-grained whole-body control. This page is a personal demo collection and will be updated as the experiments progress.
